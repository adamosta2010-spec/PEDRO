The thinking was the model, and nothing ever gave up

Two causes for the wait. The model being used for speech was the one chosen for
quality - on Gemini that is pro, which reasons silently before it answers, and
for a spoken reply that reasoning is the whole delay. Talking now uses the
quickest good model the key has, with the thinking budget set to zero.

And nothing had a time limit. If the connection stalled, the stream simply never
delivered another byte and it sat on Thinking for good - the one failure that
looks exactly like working. There is now a limit on waiting for the first word
and on the whole answer, and giving up says which happened rather than sulking.

Also fixed something I had just broken: streaming speaks each sentence as it
arrives, and the old code then spoke the entire answer again at the end. It now
waits for the last sentence to finish instead.
