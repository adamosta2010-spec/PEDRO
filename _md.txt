Settings and Teach were opening behind the dashboard, and answers now start sooner

Tapping Settings or Teach did nothing visible because the panels sit at z-index
31 and the dashboard, which is opaque and fills the screen, sits at 70 - so they
opened underneath it. Checked by rendering the page and reading the values, not
by reading the CSS. The overlay and panels now sit above every full-screen view,
the website's drawer was raised with them, and the layering guard has been
taught the new order so it cannot drift back.

Speed: the voice path never streamed. It waited for the whole answer and then
started speaking, which is where the wait was. It now reads the answer as it
arrives and speaks each finished sentence while the rest is still being written,
queued so they never overlap, and asks for a shorter answer when it is going to
be spoken aloud.

Simulations happen in the ball now rather than on another screen: the globe
fades back, whatever he built fades in inside the same circle, and tapping it
brings the globe back. It is still sandboxed with no way into the app, and he is
asked for something small that fits a circle so it arrives quickly.
