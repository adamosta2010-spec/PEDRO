He says when he has stopped talking, instead of being timed

The native voice had no way of reporting that it had finished, so the app waited
out an estimate based on the length of the sentence - which is the pause after
every answer. The synthesiser now says when it stops, the app listens for that,
and the estimate stays only as a safety net. Picking the microphone back up
happens sooner too.
