A reactor you can actually see, a dock, and less waiting

The middle of the screen was black because the core borrowed an icon that was
not drawing. It is drawn here now: a blue ball lit from inside, with a glow, a
ring of reactor segments turning around it, and its own behaviour for listening,
thinking and talking.

Along the bottom there is a dock - voice, look, teach, settings - and the voice
button opens a panel with the things worth changing without going hunting: which
voice, volume, speed, and listening in the background.

The pause after you stop talking is down from 1.7 seconds to 0.9 - long enough
to read as a pause, short enough not to feel like a wait.

And the ElevenLabs voice is the new one you picked. Anyone already carrying the
old id is moved across on first run rather than being stuck with it.
