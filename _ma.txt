The reactor keeps moving

The ball is lit in every state now, not only while he talks - idle just breathes
more slowly. Inside the glass a swirl turns, so it reads as spinning rather than
sitting there. Around it: a dial of fine ticks, and three tilted orbits at
different speeds, each carrying a light around its own path. Listening brightens
the orbits, thinking speeds everything up.

Anyone who has asked their phone for less motion gets the glow without the spin.
