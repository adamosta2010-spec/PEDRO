Counting to a thousand no longer opens the camera

Counting split badly: any sentence starting with count was treated as a question
about the room, so asking him to count to a thousand pointed the camera at the
ceiling. It now splits three ways - a number sequence needs no eyes, a thing in
front of you does, and how many X in Y is a question about the world.

  count to 1000                    -> normal
  count the boxes                  -> camera
  how many boxes can you see       -> camera
  how many days in a year          -> normal
