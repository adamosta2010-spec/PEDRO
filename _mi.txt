An estimate while he learns, and a microphone that knows what to expect

Learning was one request and a shrug. It is now three passes over different
ground - the basics, how it works, and using it in practice - which is both
better groundwork and something an estimate can be given for. The estimate is
measured rather than invented: each pass updates a running average, so a fresh
install says about twenty-one seconds and it becomes truer the more he learns.
While he works, the screen counts down and says which pass he is on, and at the
end he says how long it actually took.

The microphone was running on defaults. It is now told that it is being spoken
to rather than dictated at, to punctuate what it hears, and - most usefully -
which words to expect: his own name first, since mishearing that is the
difference between answering and ignoring you, along with yours and whatever he
has recently studied.
