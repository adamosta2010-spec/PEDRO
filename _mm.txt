Hands on the ball, readouts only when asked, and the small things

One finger on the ball turns it. Two fingers pinch it larger or smaller, or
carry it somewhere else. It cannot be pinched down to nothing, and where it is
left is remembered. Tested with real pointer events rather than by reading the
code: a drag produced rotateY(48deg), a pinch took it from 276 to 345 pixels,
and two fingers moving together shifted it by thirty across and a hundred up.

The readouts are gone from the screen until asked for by name - transcript,
memory, systems, brain, clock - and once shown each can be picked up with a
finger and put where it suits. They stay where they were put. One of five
showing by default, which is the ball on its own.

And the small things answer instantly, with no request to anyone: a timer that
counts down on the dashboard and speaks when it finishes, a coin, a die. Jokes,
riddles and a verse of a song are things he is now told to do properly when
asked rather than explaining that he is an assistant.

The ElevenLabs voice has a name now - AI - and the dock says which voice is
actually being used.
