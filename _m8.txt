Simulations and visual explanations

Ask him to show you how something works, to simulate it, animate it or plot it,
and he writes a small self-contained page and runs it - a bouncing ball, a four
stroke engine, the water cycle - while explaining it out loud in a sentence.

What he writes runs in a sandboxed frame with no same-origin access and a
content policy that blocks every kind of fetch, so a generated page can draw in
its own box and reach nothing else: not the app, not the keys, not the memory.

Questions that want a fact still get a fact - only asking to be shown, drawn,
animated, simulated or plotted opens the panel.
