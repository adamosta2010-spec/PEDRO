A wireframe globe, turning against itself

The orb is a sphere now: twelve meridians turned about the vertical axis and
seven latitude rings stacked up it, the two layers spinning in opposite
directions at different speeds, with the lit core inside and the orbits and dial
still going around the outside. Listening brightens the lines, thinking speeds
the whole thing up.

Sized from one value - the smaller of a comfortable size, most of the width, or
a third of the height - so it fits a phone without crowding the panels.

The latitude rings were written with percentage lengths, which translateZ does
not accept: every one of them would have collapsed into the middle and the
sphere would have been flat. They are driven off the orb's own size instead, and
a check now fails the build if a percentage creeps back in.
