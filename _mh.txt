Ask for what you want open, talk to a simulation, and a much shorter prompt

The dock is gone. Say open settings, teach, memory, camera, or close that, and
it happens - checked against ordinary questions so that asking what the capital
of France is does not open a panel, and open safari still opens Safari. A double
tap on the ball opens settings if speaking is not an option.

While a simulation is playing you can ask about it. The question goes to what is
on screen rather than starting a new one, and the answer appears on a small card
over the animation as well as being spoken, so you do not lose what you were
watching.

Speed: the instructions sent before every spoken word were 2374 characters of
rules about writing code and formatting markdown - none of which matters when
the answer is going to be said aloud. Speaking now sends 341. That is 86 per
cent less to read before he can start talking, on top of the quick model and the
thinking budget already set to zero.
