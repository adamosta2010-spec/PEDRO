The black screen, and studying a subject

The screen was black because hfOpen returned before it ever showed the
dashboard - no microphone, or no API key - and the chat shell is hidden in the
app, so nothing was drawn at all. I checked this by rendering the page and
measuring it rather than reasoning about the CSS: with the dashboard closed the
only things on the page were off-screen panels and a body the colour of the
black he was seeing. The layout itself was fine at phone size all along.

The dashboard is now shown first and any problem is reported inside it, with a
safety net a second and a half after boot that forces it on if anything else
ever bails out early.

And he can be sent to learn something. Tell him to go and learn about
electronics and he works through it once, keeps fourteen short notes, and says
he is ready. Ask about it later and those notes go in front of him, with an
instruction to say plainly when they do not cover what you asked. Forget
everything about a subject drops it. What he has studied shows on the dashboard
and travels in the backup file.
