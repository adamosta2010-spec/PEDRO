Background listening stopped after the first answer

Saying his name from outside the app worked once: he said yeah, and then went
quiet for good. Speaking stops the microphone, and stopping it told the native
side that listening was no longer wanted - which switched off the one thing that
can restart it while the app is off screen, leaving the job to JavaScript, which
iOS throttles precisely then.

Speaking now pauses the microphone without giving up on it, and the synthesiser
starts it again itself the moment it stops talking. The whole cycle - hear,
answer, hear again - happens in Swift and no longer depends on the web layer
being awake.
