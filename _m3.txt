Highlighting on the camera, the right picture, and a voice you can hear from the background

Three faults and one new thing.

Asking about the camera sent the whole conversation, old photos and all, so a
question about fans in a PC came back describing someone in a blue shirt. A
camera question is now its own small conversation - one question, one picture -
and requests in general carry only the two most recent images.

With the camera open every question got a picture attached, so counting to a
number came back with a description of the room as well. Abstract questions now
answer without one.

The web view's speech engine is suspended while the app is off screen, so
answers queued up until it was opened. Speaking now goes through the app's own
voice, which plays on the audio session that is already keeping it alive - with
the chosen voice, speed and volume.

New: ask him to highlight, point out or find something and he draws it on the
picture, labelled, rather than describing where it is.

And the test runner was lying. Every suite was piped to tail, so a failing suite
printed its failures and the run still exited zero. With pipefail it caught
three suites that had been broken and quiet.
