A dashboard instead of a chat app, a better voice, and a model that stays loaded

The app is now a readout: the reactor in the middle, panels either side for the
time, what is answering, whether it can answer offline, what it remembers and
what the microphone is doing, and a transcript that writes your words while you
are still speaking rather than after you stop. There is no message box in the
app at all - the same file is still served as a website, where voice cannot
work, so the chat shell stays there.

Talking has an animation of its own: bars around the core that move only while
he is speaking, so it is obvious who is talking.

The ElevenLabs voice is wired up with the voice from the library link. It needs
a key of your own - Settings has a field for it - and anything that goes wrong,
including no key and no signal, drops back to the phone's own voice rather than
leaving silence.

Speed: the on-device session was being rebuilt for every single question, which
is the pause before each answer. It is kept now, warmed up at startup, and only
rebuilt if the instructions change. The model on the PC is told to put itself on
the graphics card and stay loaded for half an hour. And a spoken conversation no
longer sends the whole history every turn - the recent exchanges are enough,
since the memory system carries anything that matters.
