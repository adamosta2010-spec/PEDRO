Moving it was laggy and left its words behind

Pinching changed the ball's width. A width change makes the browser lay out the
whole thing again - twelve meridians, seven latitude rings, a dial and three
orbits, all animating - on every single finger movement. That is the lag. And
the move was applied to the ball alone, so its words stayed where they were,
which is the off-centre look.

Both come from the same fix: one transform on the whole middle, translating and
scaling together, written once a frame rather than once per event. Scaling costs
the browser nothing; resizing costs it everything.

Checked with real pointer events: the ball and its label both moved by exactly
40 across and 40 up, and the ball's own width never changed - 276.078px before
and after.
