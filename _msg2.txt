Two causes, both about the audio session. The microphone session uses
measurement mode, which turns off output processing and leaves anything he says
almost inaudible - and the session was still open when the answer came back. He
now lets go of the microphone before speaking, and the session uses spokenAudio,
which still recognises speech and plays properly.

Volume is now its own setting with a slider that speaks a sample when you let
go, and it travels in the backup file.
