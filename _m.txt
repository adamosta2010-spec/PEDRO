Speaking speed, and listening that survives leaving the app

Background listening leaned on JavaScript to restart the microphone after each
utterance, and iOS throttles that the moment the app is off screen - so it went
quiet after the first thing you said. The recogniser now restarts itself in
Swift while background mode is on, which does not depend on the web layer at all.

Called by name while background listening is on, he answers out loud and leaves
you where you are: the machinery runs with its screen closed, so being answered
no longer means being pulled into an app.

Speaking speed is now a slider, capped between 0.7 and 1.3 - past that it stops
sounding like speech - and it travels in the backup file with the volume.
