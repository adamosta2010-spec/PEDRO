It was reading headings out loud

Asked what two plus two is, he answered "maths" and then "four". The reply had a
heading above the answer, and preparing text for speech stripped the hashes off
it but left the word behind - so a label meant for the eye was read out as if it
were part of the answer. Headings are now dropped whole, including the short
bold lines models use as headings, and he is told not to label answers at all.
