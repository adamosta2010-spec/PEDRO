Pause and stop, the microphone comes back, building only when asked, and the phone can look for itself

After one spoken answer he went deaf. Stopping the microphone did not clear the
marker that says listening is already running, and starting it again is a no-op
while that marker is set - so the next thing said was never heard. Cleared where
it is stopped, and listening is now actually started again when he finishes
speaking rather than only being labelled as listening.

Say stop and he stops - speech cut off, request abandoned, back to listening.
Say pause and he holds until told to carry on.

Building only happens when asked to build, simulate, animate or model something.
Asking how a jet engine works is a question again, and gets an answer.

The simulation was drawing into a 300 by 150 canvas - the browser default, most
of it outside the circle, which is why nothing appeared. The wrapper sizes it
now, and only when the code has not sized it itself, since resizing a canvas
wipes what is on it. Tested by running a real generated page through the whole
pipeline: 110,730 pixels painted where there had been none.

And the phone can look at a picture on its own. The language model reads text
only, but the phone has had image recognition built in for years: it names what
it sees, reads any words, counts faces, and needs no key and no signal. It says
plainly that those are its own eyes rather than a proper model.
