Texting by voice, and letting him change his own code behind your fingerprint

Say text mum saying I will be late and Messages opens with the words already in
it. A number said out loud is dialled into the recipient; without one you pick
who it goes to. He cannot send it and should not - that press is yours.

And he can change himself. Say edit yourself so that... and he writes the
smallest piece of JavaScript that does it, shows you the code, and asks for your
face before keeping it. What he writes runs inside the app with everything the
app can reach, which is exactly why it is gated: this is the one thing in here
that can do real damage.

Three safeguards, because a bad edit could otherwise be unrecoverable. You see
the code before it is kept. Saying undo that change takes the last one back. And
if a change stops him starting, his changes are switched off automatically on
the next run and he says so, rather than leaving an app that will not open.
