Building an engine took too long and then gave up

It waited for the entire reply - including whatever the model wrote after the
code - and abandoned the attempt at twenty-five seconds, which is roughly how
long a careful answer takes. So the usual outcome was a wait and then nothing.

It reads the code as it arrives now and stops the moment the block closes, since
everything after that is chatter. The ask is shorter: under forty lines, keep it
in the circle, let the parts be dragged. While it writes, the screen says how
many lines have arrived rather than sitting still. And what it builds is kept -
asking for the same thing again puts it up instantly.
